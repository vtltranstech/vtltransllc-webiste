Fleet Invoice AI

AI-powered expense and paperwork management for trucking fleets.

Fleet Invoice AI turns a driver's phone into a complete expense and document system — receipts get read automatically by AI, Bills of Lading and Proof of Delivery get scanned as clean PDFs, and every submission alerts the office by WhatsApp and email in real time.

What It Does

🧾

Automatic Receipt Scanning

Scans receipts and reads vendor, amount, date, and category automatically.

📄

BOL & POD Capture

Captures multi-page Bill of Lading / Proof of Delivery documents as clean PDF.

📊

Live Spend Dashboard

Gives fleet managers a live spend dashboard, full ledger, and audit trail.

🔔

Instant Alerts

Sends instant alerts to WhatsApp and email whenever a driver submits something.

🔒

Secure by Company

Keeps every company's data fully separate and secure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get access?

Your fleet administrator sets up your account and gives you a Company Code to register with in the app.

I forgot my PIN — what do I do?

Contact your fleet administrator to reset your account.

Is my company's data visible to other companies using the app?

No. Every company's data is fully isolated and never shared or visible across accounts.
